# AngelCare Home-Care Assessment — Tier 1 Quick Questionnaire (EN, v1 2026-08-08)

**Group vision (in-home care, confirmed by Wilson 2026-08-08):** "Let the elderly live with dignity and autonomy in the familiar surroundings they know, until the very end." — DoctorNow Home (medical side) + AngelCare (caregiving side) serve this vision together. This questionnaire belongs to the caregiving side: supporting ageing in place, while honestly acknowledging that in some situations a residential care home is the best choice.

**Positioning:** A family decision-support tool before SCNAMES (not a medical diagnosis)
**Length:** 17 questions (15 scored + 2 background) · approx 6-8 minutes · can pause and resume
**Scoring principle:** Option B — each dimension scored independently → 🟢/🟡/🔴 flags; **no cross-dimension totals or weighting**
**Entry line:** "17 questions · ~8 minutes · save & resume anytime · get a personalised recommendation on completion"

---

## Background questions (not scored — for report context)

**C1. How old is the elder?**
- 60-69 / 70-79 / 80-89 / 90 or above

**C2. What is the elder's current living arrangement?**
- Lives alone / With family / With a domestic helper / Other

---

## Group 1: Daily self-care (3 questions) → maps to Barthel ADL + Lawton IADL

**Q1. Can the elder feed, dress and use the toilet by themselves?**
- Fully independent (0)
- Needs some help (1)
- Needs help with most tasks (2)

**Q2. Can the elder move around, and transfer between bed and chair, by themselves?**
- Independent mobility and transfers (0)
- Needs a mobility aid or minimal help (1)
- Needs someone's help or cannot move around (2)

**Q3. Can the elder manage everyday tasks by themselves (shopping, cooking, taking medication, making phone calls)?**
- Fully independent (0)
- Needs help with some tasks (1)
- Needs help with most tasks or unable to manage (2)

**Flag rule (score 0-6):** 🟢 0-1 Good / 🟡 2-3 Needs attention / 🔴 4-6 Significant impairment
**Tier 2 deepening:** Full 10-item ADL (Barthel) + 7-item IADL (Lawton)
**Trigger:** Self-care 🟡 → optional deep-dive; Self-care 🔴 → recommended + professional assessment

---

## Group 2: Cognition & behaviour (3 questions) → maps to AD8 + NPI-Q

**Q4. Has the elder's memory clearly declined in recent months (repeating the same questions, forgetting recent events)?**
- No (0) / Sometimes (1) / Clearly declined (2)

**Q5. Does the elder show any of these behaviours: day-night reversal, wandering, agitation, aggression, suspiciousness or hallucinations?**
- No (0) / Occasionally (1) / Often (2)

**Q6. Can the elder stay home alone safely for a period of time (won't forget to turn off the stove, get lost, or open the door to strangers)?**
- Can stay alone safely (0) / Needs reminders (1) / Cannot be left alone (2)

**Flag rule (score 0-6):** 🟢 0 / 🟡 1-2 / 🔴 3-6
**Red-flag override:** Q5 = 2 (frequent behavioural symptoms) → **directly triggers** NPI-Q module + home-safety risk warning (dementia/BPSD red flag); Q6 = 2 (cannot stay alone safely) → home-safety risk warning + NPI-Q/AD8 offer
**Tier 2 deepening:** 8-item AD8 → if positive, triggers 12-domain NPI-Q

---

## Group 3: Family support (3 questions) → maps to care resources / Zarit Burden concept

**Q7. Is there anyone (family or helper) available to care for the elder?**
- Someone available full-time (0) / Someone available but working or part-time (1) / Essentially no one (2)

**Q8. How heavy is the carer's time and physical burden?**
- Manageable with ease (0) / Somewhat straining but manageable (1) / Under great stress already (2)

**Q9. Can the family afford professional care services (e.g. day centre, home nursing)?**
- Can afford (0) / Needs subsidy or finds it somewhat difficult (1) / Difficult to afford (2)

**Flag rule (score 0-6):** 🟢 0-1 Support adequate / 🟡 2-3 Borderline / 🔴 4-6 Support insufficient
**Tier 2 deepening:** 7-item family care resource assessment + community resource list (CCSV / IHCS / day centres)
**Trigger:** 🟡 → optional; 🔴 → recommended + professional assessment

---

## Group 4: Home safety (3 questions) → maps to CDC STEADI / home fall-prevention check

**Q10. Has the elder fallen in the past year?**
- No (0) / Once (1) / Twice or more (2)

**Q11. Are the non-slip measures, grab rails and lighting at home adequate (hallway, bathroom, stairs)?**
- Adequate (0) / Some areas need improvement (1) / Clearly inadequate (2)

**Q12. Are there obvious safety hazards in the bathroom and stairs (no grab rails, slippery, clutter blocking the way)?**
- None (0) / A few (1) / Multiple (2)

**Flag rule (score 0-6):** 🟢 0 / 🟡 1-2 / 🔴 3-6
**Trigger:** Q10 = 2 (two or more falls) → strongly recommends full 11-item safety module; 🟡 → optional
**Tier 2 deepening:** 11-item home environment safety assessment

---

## Group 5: Mood (3 questions) → maps to GDS-15

**Q13. Has the elder been feeling low, tearful or gloomy recently?**
- No (0) / Sometimes (1) / Often (2)

**Q14. Has the elder lost interest in things they used to enjoy (hobbies, gatherings, going out)?**
- No (0) / Sometimes (1) / Often (2)

**Q15. Have the elder's sleep or appetite changed noticeably (insomnia/sleepiness, reduced food intake)?**
- No (0) / Mild (1) / Noticeable (2)

**Flag rule (score 0-6):** 🟢 0 / 🟡 1-2 / 🔴 3-6
**Red-flag override:** 🔴 (Q13-15 total ≥3) → regardless of quadrant, report must include mental-health intervention advice (counselling / psychiatric) + GDS-15 offer
**Tier 2 deepening:** 15-item GDS-15 (ideally self-completed by the elder; family may read questions aloud)

---

## Tier 1 → Tier 2 trigger rules (confirmed 00:18)

| Tier 1 result | Tier 2 behaviour |
|---|---|
| Any dimension 🟡 | **Offer** — show that dimension's module as "optional deep-dive" |
| Any dimension 🔴 | **Offer + recommend** — module + suggest professional assessment (SCNAMES / home assessment) |
| Q5 = 2 | **Directly trigger** NPI-Q module + home-safety risk warning |
| Q6 = 2 | Home-safety risk warning + NPI-Q/AD8 offer |
| Q10 = 2 | Strongly recommend full 11-item home-safety module |
| Mood 🔴 (Q13-15 ≥3) | Mental-health intervention advice (regardless of quadrant) + GDS-15 offer |
| All 🟢 | **No Tier 2 offers** — issue the all-clear report directly |

Tier 2 is never mandatory — severe answers surface first with stronger recommendations, but all modules remain optional deepening.

## Flags → Recommendation Engine (Option B)

**Per-dimension output:** 🟢🟡🔴 flag + one-line summary + dimension-specific advice

**Four-quadrant lean (summary only, not the engine):**
- Ability axis = worst flag among Self-care / Cognition / Mood (worst-of, conservative principle)
- Support axis = worst flag among Family / Environment (worst-of)
- Quadrant position: Ability × Support → Stay home / Home + community / Home + professional care / Consider residential care

**Lean output format (default 2 leans, with reasons):**
- **Lean 1 (primary):** direction — because [citing flag cards, 2-3 line explanation] — what it means in practice [day centre / meal delivery / IHCS / home nursing…]
- **Lean 2 (alternative):** other direction — **"choose this if…"** condition [e.g. behavioural symptoms worsen / carer can't continue shifts / high fall risk]
- Note: the official threshold still rests on professional assessment (SCNAMES)

**Lean count rules (edge cases):**
- All [Good] → only 1 lean (ageing in place, don't create doubt)
- Both axes [Need intervention] → primary = consider residential care, alternative = home + intensive professional care (short-term transition option)
- Otherwise → default 2 leans (primary + alternative-with-condition)
- Never more than 2 (avoid choice paralysis)

**Red-flag overrides (take priority over quadrant):**
1. Q5 = 2 or Q6 = 2 → home-safety risk warning (even if everything else is fine)
2. Mood [Need intervention] → mental-health intervention advice
3. Any dimension [Need intervention] → suggest contacting professional assessment (SCNAMES / home care assessment)

**Report output:** per-dimension flag cards → Lean 1 + Lean 2 (with reasons and conditions) → red-flag warnings → Tier 2 module suggestions → WhatsApp/Email CTA (score payload prefill)
